List me nahi mila? Naam se hi ban jayega

Har naye phone ke liye database update karna possible nahi. Isliye engine model ke naam ko padh kar brand, series aur suffix se specs nikaal leta hai — phir wahi formula chalta hai.

NAAM SE PADHTA HAI

Brand aur series

“Redmi Note”, “Realme Narzo”, “Galaxy M”, “iQOO Z” — har series ka apna class hai. Engine naam se series pakadta hai aur usi class ka base sens deta hai.

SUFFIX

Pro, Ultra, Neo, Plus

“Pro” ya “Ultra” dikha to phone ko upar ke tier me rakha jaata hai — zyada FPS, isliye base sens thodi neeche. “Smart”, “Spark”, “Hot”, “C-series” entry tier me jaate hain.

CONFIRM

Aap check kar lo

Estimate ke baad screen size, refresh rate aur power tier ke dropdown aapke saamne hote hain. Ek nazar dekh lo — galat lage to badal do, sens turant naya ho jayega.

HAMESHA

Ek phone, ek sens

Do players ka same phone ho, phir bhi thumb speed alag hoti hai — isliye har phone ke liye alag preset hai, par last 5–10 point tuning aapko khud karni padegi.

04

Ye values kaise nikalti hain

Random numbers nahi — ek simple rule set hai jo teen cheezon par chalta hai. Isliye har phone ka preset thoda different hota hai.

FACTOR 01

Screen size

Chhoti screen par thumb ka ek swipe zyada angle cover karta hai, isliye sens thodi kam chahiye. Badi screen par same swipe kam ghumta hai — isliye General upar jaata hai.

FACTOR 02

Refresh rate

120Hz+ screen par frame zyada smooth aate hain, drag predictable hota hai, to thoda kam sens par bhi control milta hai. 60Hz par chhoti galtiyan zyada dikhti hain.

FACTOR 03

Chipset tier

Entry-level phone par FPS girta rehta hai — usme high sens se drag “skippy” lagta hai, isliye base sens upar rakhi jaati hai. Flagship par base sens neeche.

DERIVED

Scope ladder

Red Dot = General −5, 2x = General −15, 4x = General −30, AWM ≈ General × 0.3, Free Look ≈ General × 0.6. Isse close se long range tak control girta nahi.

05

Preset lagane ke baad

Download karne ke baad ye 4 cheezein karo — headshot consistency isi se aati hai.

STEP 01

FPS lock karo

Graphics smooth/low, High FPS on. Frame girta rahega to koi bhi sens consistent nahi lagegi.

STEP 02

Fire button set karo

Button ko thumb ke natural reach par rakho aur uske thoda upar khali jagah chhodo — one-tap headshot ke liye drag space zaroori hai.

STEP 03

Training me 10 min

Dummy ke around circle maaro. Crosshair target se aage nikalta hai to General −5, peeche rehta hai to +5.

STEP 04

10–15 match tak na badlo

Muscle memory isi se banti hai. Har match me badalna sabse bada time waste hai.

06

Chhote sawaal

Free Fire India aur Free Fire MAX me same preset chalega?

Haan. Dono games me sensitivity system same hai — 6 sliders, 0 se 100. Ek hi preset dono me daal sakte ho. Farq sirf app ke weight ka hai: MAX me graphics heavy hone se FPS kam girta hai, to kamzor phone par General 5 point upar rakhna comfortable lagta hai.

Ye sensitivity headshot guarantee karti hai?

Nahi — koi bhi sens headshot guarantee nahi karti. Sens sirf aapke haath ki speed ko game ke saath match karti hai. Headshot ka asli kaam HUD layout, fire button ki jagah aur drag ki practice karti hai. Isliye download ke saath HUD tips bhi diye jaate hain.

DPI kitna rakhna chahiye?

Free Fire ke andar DPI slider nahi hota — DPI aapke phone/emulator level ki setting hai. Is site me har phone ke liye ek suggested DPI range di gayi hai. Pehle in-game sliders se khelo, DPI sabse last me chhedo.

Gyroscope se aim kar sakte hain?

Nahi. Free Fire India aur MAX dono me gyroscope aiming support nahi hai. Aim poora thumb/finger drag se hota hai, isliye sensitivity aur HUD hi aapke main tools hain.

Mera phone list me nahi hai, phir bhi sens milegi?

Search box me apne phone ka naam likh ke Enter dabao. Agar model database me nahi hai to engine naam se brand, series aur suffix padh kar specs estimate kar leta hai, aur uske hisaab se sens bana deta hai. Result panel me screen size, refresh rate aur power tier ke dropdown se aap use correct bhi kar sakte ho.

Subscribe karne ke baad dobara popup aayega?

Nahi, ek baar unlock karne ke baad isi browser me dobara nahi aayega. Agar browser ka data clear kar doge to popup phir aa sakta hai.
